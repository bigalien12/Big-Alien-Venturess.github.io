BIG ALIEN VENTURE 👽 — READY WEBSITE

IMPORTANT:
Keep index.html and the images folder together.

TO OPEN:
1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Open index.html.
3. If you upload it to a web host, upload the whole folder, including images.

FEATURES:
- Big Alien Enterprise logo
- Customer welcome/login
- Product browsing/search
- Buy Now and cart
- Bank transfer and pay-on-delivery checkout
- Customer order history
- Customer shipping progress
- WhatsApp customer service: 07025136166
- Owner product management
- Owner payment confirmation and delivery-status updates

OWNER:
Use the Owner section to create your owner account on the browser you use to manage the store.

SECURITY:
This is a browser/local-storage prototype. A real public store needs server-side authentication, database storage, secure password hashing, server-enforced owner permissions, and payment verification.
